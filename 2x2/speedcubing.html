📌 Basics & Notations (Speedcubing)

R (Right): Right side ko clockwise ghumayein.
L (Left): Left side ko clockwise ghumayein.
U (Up): Top layer ko clockwise ghumayein.
F (Front): Front face ko clockwise ghumayein.
' (Prime / !): Anti-clockwise ghumana (e.g., R' / R!).
2: Us side ko 2 baar ghumayein (e.g., U2).

🎨 Opposite Color Scheme (Speedcubing Rule)

Speedcubing mein opposite colors turant pehchanna zaroori hai — is se aap bina soche jaldi decide kar sakte hain ke konsi face abhi "front" ya "back" honi chahiye:

⚪ White ↔️ 🟡 Yellow
🟢 Green ↔️ 🔵 Blue
🟠 Orange ↔️ 🔴 Red
💡 Speedcubing vs Beginner Method: Beginner method mein hum hamesha White se shuru karte hain — is se seekhna aasan hota hai. Speedcubing (Ortega) method mein hum "Color Neutral" hote hain, yani jo bhi color scramble mein sabse zyada ready ho, wahi use karte hain. Is se inspection time bachta hai aur solve tez hoti hai!

🎲 Step 1: Color-Neutral First Face

Speedcubing mein har baar White se start nahi karte! Scrambled cube ko ghuma kar dekhein — jis color ke sabse zyada pieces pehle se sahi group mein judy hue hoon (e.g., agar kisi ek color ke 2-3 corners pehle se sahi jagah ke qareeb hoon), usi color ki face ko apna starting point banayein.

Baaki corners ko us face mein lagane ke liye neeche wala formula use karein. Yahan farq yeh hai: side colors ka match hona zaroori nahi — bas is face ka poora ek color se bharna zaroori hai:

R U R' U'
🔗 Practice Tip: Casual practice ke doran jaan boojh kar alag-alag color se shuru karne ki koshish karein (sirf White nahi) — is se aapki "color recognition speed" barhegi, jo asli speedcubing competitions mein bohot faayda deti hai.

☀️ Step 2: Opposite Face OLL (8 Conditions)

Pehli face poori banne ke baad, cube ko flip kar dein taake wo face neeche chali jaye. Ab uske opposite color ki face banani hai (jaise agar pehli face White thi, to ab Yellow banayenge) — is baar sirf orientation theek karni hai. Neeche di gayi 8 conditions mein se jo bhi aapke cube se match kare, wo formula lagayein:

Condition 1

R U R' U R U2 R'

Condition 2

R' U' R U' R' U2 R

Condition 3

R U2 R' U' R U' R'

Condition 4

F R U R' U' F'

Condition 5

F' R U R' U' R' F R

Condition 6

F R U R' U' R U R' U' R U R' U' F'

Condition 7

R U' R2 U R2 U R2 U' R

Condition 8

R U R' U' R' F R F'
💡 OLL = Orient Last Layer. In 8 cases ko yaad rakhna shuru mein mushkil lag sakta hai, lekin practice se aap pattern turant pehchan lenge — bilkul jaise beginner guide ke 7 yellow-face cases the, bas yahan thodi zyada.

🏆 Step 3: PBL — Permute Both Layers (5 Conditions)

Yeh Ortega method ka sabse powerful step hai — dono layers ke corners ek hi formula se sahi jagah aa jate hain, aur cube seedha solve ho jata hai! Neeche di gayi 5 conditions mein se jo match kare, wo lagayein:

Corner Condition 1

R2 F2 R2

Corner Condition 2

R' F R F' R U2 R' U R U2 R'

Corner Condition 3

R2 U' R2 U2 F2 U' R2

Corner Condition 4

R U' L U2 R' U L'

Corner Condition 5

R U' R' U' F2 U' R U R' U F2
💡 PBL = Permute Both Layers. Yeh Ortega method ki jaan hai — beginner method ke Step 2+3 (jo alag alag karte the) ko is single, tez step mein combine kar deta hai. Isi wajah se Ortega beginner method se kaafi tez hai!
