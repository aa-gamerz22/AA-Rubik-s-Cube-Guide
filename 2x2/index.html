📌 Basics & Notations (2x2 ke Liye)

💡 Important: 3x3 ke ulat, 2x2 cube mein koi fixed center piece nahi hota — har corner khud hi apni pehchaan hai. Is ka matlab, aap kisi bhi color se apna "starting face" bana sakte hain; bas jahan se shuru karein, us color ko consistent rakhein jab tak solve na ho jaye. Is guide mein hum White se shuru karenge.
R (Right): Right side ko clockwise ghumayein.
L (Left): Left side ko clockwise ghumayein.
U (Up): Top layer ko clockwise ghumayein.
F (Front): Front face ko clockwise ghumayein.
' (Prime / !): Anti-clockwise ghumana (e.g., R' / R!).
2: Us side ko 2 baar ghumayein (e.g., U2).

🔴 Step 1: Pehli Layer (First Layer) Banana

Achi khabar: 2x2 cube asal mein 3x3 cube ke sirf 8 corners hain — koi edges nahi, koi centers nahi! Is liye poori solve sirf corners ke aas-paas ghoomti hai, aur pehla step hai koi bhi 4 matching corners ka ek layer banana.

  1. Koi bhi ek White corner piece dhoondein aur usay Bottom (neeche) mein rakh dein — chahe wo abhi kisi bhi angle par ho, bas neeche layer mein hona chahiye.
  2. Ab doosra White corner piece dhoondein jiske side colors us pehle wale corner se match karte hoon (yani agar pehla corner White-Red-Blue hai, to doosra bhi unhi 2 side colors mein se kam az kam ek se milta ho), aur usay Top layer mein us jagah rakhein jahan wo neeche wale corner ke upar aa sake.
  3. Ab niche diya gaya formula tab tak lagate rahein jab tak wo Top wala corner neeche apni sahi jagah "insert" na ho jaye:
R U R' U'

Isi tarah baaki bache hue 2 corners ke sath bhi karein — har baar koi bhi 2 matching White corners lekar unhe upar-neeche set karein aur wahi formula lagayein. Is ke baad aapki First Layer mukammal ho jayegi:

🔗 Recognize this? Yeh R U R' U' wahi "sexy move" hai jo 3x3 Rubik's Cube mein bhi corners fit karne ke liye use hota hai — ek dafa yaad ho jaye to har cube mein kaam aata hai!

🟡 Step 2: Yellow Face Banana (Last Layer Orient Karna)

Ab hum bachi hui (Yellow) layer ke corners ko sahi orientation (ghumaav) mein layenge — abhi unki jagah sahi nahi honi chahiye, sirf sahi rukh (Yellow upar) hona chahiye.

  1. Cube ko is tarah pakdein ke jo layer complete ho chuki hai (White) wo ab neeche chali jaye, aur bachi hui Yellow layer upar aa jaye — kyunke hum isi upar wali layer ko dekh kar orient karenge.
  2. Dekhein ke koi Yellow piece pehle se sahi rukh (Yellow upar ki taraf) mein laga hua hai ya nahi. Jo corner sahi nahi hai, usay Front-Right-Top position mein layein:

Phir neeche wala formula lagayein — bas is baat ka khayal rakhein ke jis corner par kaam kar rahe hain wo hamesha Front-Right-Top hi rahe:

R U R' U'

⚠️ Zaroori Note: Formula ko hamesha poora lagayein — beech mein na rukein, chahe corner sahi lagta bhi dikhe. Jab ek corner sahi ho jaye (Yellow upar), sirf top layer (U) ghumakar agla unsolved corner Front-Right-Top mein layein — neeche wali White layer ko bilkul na chhuein — phir dobara R U R' U' lagayein. Yeh process har corner ke liye dohrayein.

🏆 Step 3: Aakhri Qadam — Corners Ko Sahi Jagah Lana

Yellow face poori ban chuki hai, lekin corners abhi shayad sahi position mein nahi hain (sirf sahi rukh mein hain). Ab hum unhe permute (position swap) karke cube mukammal solve karenge. Is ke liye 2 mumkin cases hoti hain:

Case 1

Is case mein koi bhi 2 corners aapas mein match nahi kar rahe (koi bhi corner apni sahi jagah par nahi hai).

Cube ko kisi bhi taraf se pakar kar (koi bhi face Front rakh kar) neeche wala formula lagayein:

R B' R F2 R' B R F2 R2

Is ke baad aapko Case 2 mil jayegi!

Case 2

Is case mein 2 corners ka color aapas mein match kar raha hota hai (ek dusre ke barabar/adjacent lag rahe hain).

In matching corners ko FRONT par rakh kar wahi formula dobara lagayein:

R B' R F2 R' B R F2 R2
🔗 Recognize this too? Yeh bilkul wahi formula hai jo 3x3 Rubik's Cube ke last layer corners set karne ke liye use hota hai — 2x2 seekhne ka fayda yeh hai ke aap 3x3 ke liye bhi taiyar ho rahe hain!
