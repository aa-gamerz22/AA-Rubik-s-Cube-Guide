📌 Basics & Notations (Ghoomane ke Tareeqe)

R (Right): Right side ko clockwise ghumayein.
L (Left): Left side ko clockwise ghumayein.
U (Up): Top layer ko clockwise ghumayein.
F (Front): Front face ko clockwise ghumayein.
' (Prime / !): Anti-clockwise ghumana (e.g., R' / R!).
2: Us side ko 2 baar ghumayein (e.g., U2).

🔴 Step 1: First Layer (Pehli Layer)

Pehli layer ko solve karne ke 2 main steps hote hain: White Cross aur White Corners.

1. The White Cross (White Plus Banana)

  1. Pehle Top (Yellow Center) par ek Daisy pattern banayein (Center Yellow ho aur uske chaaron taraf White edges hoon).
  2. Har White edge piece ke doosre color ko uske matching Center piece se milayein (e.g., White-Red edge ko Red center se).
  3. Phir us face ko 2 baar ghumayein (F2) taake White edge neeche (White Center ke paas) chala jaye.
  4. Chaaron edges ke liye yehi karein. Aapka White Cross ban jayega!

2. White Corners (Kone Fit Karna)

  1. Cube ko ulta pakdein taake White Cross Neeche (Bottom) ho.
  2. Bottom layer ke corners ko fill karne ke liye Top layer mein White corner pieces dhoondein.
  3. Corner piece ko uske sahi centers ke beech mein layein (e.g., agar corner White-Blue-Red hai, to use Blue aur Red center ke beech rakhein).
  4. Sahi jagah fit karne ke liye niche diya gaya 4-Step Algorithm tab tak lagayein jab tak White piece neeche na chala jaye:
Corner Algorithm:
R U R' U'

(Agar White piece neeche pehle se galat jagah par hai, to us par bhi yehi algorithm lagayein taake wo bahar nikal aaye).

🟢 Step 2: Second Layer (Doosri Layer)

Top layer se aise edge pieces dhoondein jinme Yellow color na ho. Edge ko uske matching center ke sath ek T-shape banayein.

1. Edge ko RIGHT side mein dalne ke liye:

U R U' R' U' F' U F

2. Edge ko LEFT side mein dalne ke liye:

R' U' R' U' R' U R U R

3. Switch / Misoriented Edge (Galat Direction/Jagah):

R U2 R' U R U2 R' U F' U' F

🟡 Step 3: Third Layer (Yellow Cross & Full Yellow Face)

Yellow face par plus (cross) banane ke liye ye steps karein:

1. Dot (Sirf Center Yellow):

F R U R' U' F'

(Isse Arrow ban jayega)

2. Arrow (L-Shape):

Arrow ko Top-Left position mein rakhein, phir algorithm lagayein:

F R U R' U' R U R' U' F'

(Isse Horizontal Line ban jayegi)

3. Line (Left to Right Horizontal):

Line ko hamesha horizontal (L to R) rakhein:

F R U R' U' F'

✨ Yellow Cross ke Baad Full Yellow Face Banana (All 7 Cases)

Yellow cross banne ke baad aapke paas neeche diye gaye 7 cases mein se koi ek position aayegi. Unhe in formulas se solve karein:

Case 1 (Main Face 1)

R' U2 R U R' U R

Case 2 (Main Face 2)

U' R U2 R' U' R U' R'

Case 3

Formula: [Face 2] U2 [Face 1]

U' R U2 R' U' R U' R' U2 R' U2 R U R' U R

Case 4

Formula: [Face 2] [Face 1]

U' R U2 R' U' R U' R' R' U2 R U R' U R

Case 5

Formula: [Face 2] U [Face 1]

U' R U2 R' U' R U' R' U R' U2 R U R' U R

Case 6

Formula: [Face 1] U' [Face 1]

R' U2 R U R' U R U' R' U2 R U R' U R

Case 7

Formula: [Face 1] [Face 1]

R' U2 R U R' U R R' U2 R U R' U R

🔶 Step 4: Yellow Corners ko Sahi Jagah Lagana

Ab hum Top layer ke corners ke matching colors ko sahi face par layenge.

Case 1: Agar 2 Corners ka color same ho (Headlights/Matching Corners)

Jaisa ke `last-corner-1-main.png` mein dikhaya gaya hai, 2 same corners ko FRONT side par rakhein aur ye formula lagayein:

R B' R F2 R' B R F2 R2

Case 2: Agar koi bhi same color corner na mile

Kisi bhi side se upar wala formula (`R B' R F2 R' B R F2 R2`) lagayein. Phir 2 same corners milne par unhe FRONT par karke dobara yehi formula lagayein.

🏆 Step 5: Last Edges Position (Cube Final Solve)

Akhiri step mein edges ko unki sahi jagah set karke cube solve ho jayega!

Main Formula:

R U' R U R U R U' R' U' R2

Case 1

Aisa setup ho to Main Formula 1 Baar lagayein:

R U' R U R U R U' R' U' R2 ×1

Case 2

Aisa setup ho to Main Formula 2 Baar lagayein:

R U' R U R U R U' R' U' R2 ×2

Case 3

Aisa setup ho to Main Formula 3 Baar lagayein:

R U' R U R U R U' R' U' R2 ×3
